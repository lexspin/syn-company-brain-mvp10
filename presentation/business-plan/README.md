# SYN business plan

Editorial edition 1.1 · Alexandru Rada · 13 October 2026

[Read on GitHub](SYN-BUSINESS-PLAN.md) · [PDF](SYN-BUSINESS-PLAN.pdf) · [Editable Word](SYN-BUSINESS-PLAN.docx) · [MVP10 presentation](../../README.md)

The short plan connects personal Company Brain continuity to a supported service, team integration and a cooperative horizon. Eight annexes develop the protocols, dependencies, trust, arbitration, security and evaluation mechanisms. The edition includes 30 source references, five explanatory footnotes, six figures and a subject and notation index.

[![SYN conditional development path](figures/visual-development.png)](SYN-BUSINESS-PLAN.md#development)

The [illustrated reader](SYN-BUSINESS-PLAN.html) and [3D model](SYN-3D-MAP.html) are self-contained local web companions. GitHub displays their source; serve the presentation folder locally to use rotation, selectable nodes and linked explanations. The 3D layout expresses conceptual relationships.

The plan's MVP, V1 and V2 are business stages. **MVP10 Presentation** is the sharing edition of the current software and its supporting work. Wider stages develop through the conditions in the [dependency register](SYN-BUSINESS-PLAN.md#register).

[Research and development papers](../papers/README.md) provide the architectural lineage and the latest dated development annexes.
