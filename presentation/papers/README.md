## How to read the lineage

The framework establishes the architectural proposition. The research continuation develops semantic and procedural continuity. The two 13 October annexes describe successive development reviews, with their own evidence dates and acceptance boundaries. The SYN plan places this work within a supported-service business and a longer cooperative horizon.

The original edition dates, figure captions and review-draft status remain visible. MVP10 names the presentation package; it does not relabel historical screenshots or convert proposed evaluations into measured results. Tests reported in a paper belong to that paper's recorded run. Current branch verification is documented in the [MVP10 presentation](../../README.md).
